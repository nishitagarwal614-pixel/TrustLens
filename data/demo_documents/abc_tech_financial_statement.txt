ABC TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED (NSE: ABCTECH, BSE: 543210)
ANNUAL AUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS
Document: Statement of Audited Standalone & Consolidated Financial Results
Filing Date: October 24, 2024
Source: NSE India Corporate Disclosures

FINANCIAL REVIEW & GUIDANCE SUMMARY:
1. Operational Results:
ABC Tech reported consolidated revenues of INR 1,250 Crore for the trailing quarter, reflecting an annualized organic growth rate of 4.2% year-over-year. Operating free cash flow generation reached INR 185 Crore.

2. Management Clarification on Speculative Social Media Assertions:
The Board of Directors explicitly notes that reports on unofficial social channels suggesting an imminent 30% or 50% weekly stock surge or secret high-margin defence acquisition contracts are speculative and unverified. ABC Technologies complies with SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR) Regulation 30, and confirms that all material price-sensitive information is made public through formal stock exchange feeds.

3. Dividend & Investor Guidance:
The Board approved an interim dividend of INR 2.50 per equity share. Future financial performance depends on enterprise IT spending and global cloud migration cycles.
