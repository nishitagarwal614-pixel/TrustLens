TATA MOTORS LIMITED (NSE: TATAMOTORS, BSE: 500570)
REGULATORY FILING & INVESTOR PRESENTATION
Document: Quarterly Operational and Financial Performance Review
Filing Date: November 12, 2024
Source: BSE Corporate Announcement Section

FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL METRICS:
1. Electric Vehicle (EV) Business Performance:
Tata Passenger Electric Mobility Ltd (TPEM) reported quarterly EV wholesales of 15,642 units, delivering a year-over-year volume growth of 12.3%. EV penetration in the company's domestic passenger vehicle portfolio reached 13.8%. Total revenue from the EV segment stood at INR 2,140 Crore.

2. Margin and Capital Expenditure:
Operating EBITDA margin for the commercial and passenger vehicle segments improved to 11.4%. The company confirmed ongoing capital expenditure of INR 8,000 Crore towards battery pack localization, Gen-3 dedicated EV architectures (Avinya), and Sanand manufacturing plant expansion.

3. Forward Disclaimer:
Automotive product cycles and margins remain vulnerable to semiconductor chip supplies, battery raw material indices (Lithium and Nickel), and global macroeconomic pressures. The company issues no guarantees of equity valuation multipliers or predetermined shareholder yield.
