RESERVE BANK OF INDIA (RBI)
MONETARY POLICY STATEMENT & RESOLUTION
Document: Resolution of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)
Release Date: December 08, 2024
Source: Reserve Bank of India Communications Department

MONETARY POLICY DECISION & MACROECONOMIC ASSESSMENT:
1. Policy Repo Rate:
The Monetary Policy Committee decided by a majority of 5 to 1 to keep the policy repo rate under the liquidity adjustment facility (LAF) unchanged at 6.50 per cent. Consequently, the standing deposit facility (SDF) rate remains at 6.25 per cent and the marginal standing facility (MSF) rate and the Bank Rate at 6.75 per cent.

2. Impact on Borrowing Costs & Banking Transmission:
An increase or persistence in interest rates directly affects bank lending rates, repo-linked external benchmark lending rates (EBLR), and retail borrowing costs including auto loans and home mortgage EMIs. Such rate environments typically influence capital-intensive, rate-sensitive sectors like real estate, construction, and consumer durables while supporting net interest margins for high-CASA deposit lenders.

3. Inflation Target:
CPI headline inflation is projected at 5.4 per cent for 2024-25, with risks evenly balanced. The MPC remains focused on withdrawal of accommodation to ensure that inflation progressively aligns to the 4.0 per cent target while supporting economic growth.
