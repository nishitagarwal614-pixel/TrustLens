SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA (SEBI)
CIRCULAR / REGULATORY DIRECTIVE
Document: Regulatory Framework for Financial Influencers ("Finfluencers") and Unregistered Investment Advisers
Circular No: SEBI/HO/MIRSD/2023/142
Date: August 25, 2023
Jurisdiction: Indian Capital Markets

PROVISIONS ON CLAIMS AND PROMOTIONAL DISCLOSURES:
1. Prohibition of Guaranteed Return Assurances:
Under Regulation 4 of the SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices) Regulations, no person or intermediary shall promise, assure, or guarantee specific percentage returns (e.g., "guaranteed 30%", "risk-free doubling", "sure-shot profit") on equity shares, derivatives, or mutual fund instruments. Capital markets carry inherent market risk.

2. Mandatory Sponsorship & Affiliation Disclosures:
Any entity, social media creator, or financial analyst receiving compensation, referral fees, revenue-share incentives, or affiliate bonuses for mentioning financial platforms, brokerage apps, or algorithmic advisory tools must prominently display clear, unambiguous disclosures such as "#Advertisement", "#Sponsored", or "Paid Partnership" at the start of the video, caption, or post.

3. Unregistered Advisory Ban:
Operating closed VIP groups, Telegram channels, WhatsApp tip groups, or subscription portals giving direct buy/sell target prices and execution timings without SEBI Research Analyst (RA) or Investment Adviser (IA) registration is strictly prohibited under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

4. Investor Redressal:
Investors misled by unregistered financial advisors or fraudulent return promises are encouraged to lodge formal complaints on the SEBI SCORES portal (scores.gov.in).
